## Rules for the next one

- Do not reuse: bus stop, bedroom phone call, diner booth, laundromat, "sees the future",
  "the other person is the reason you're waiting", "an overdue library book".
- The library is the recurring world, not a one-off set: reuse it deliberately. Meera and Dev
  return; the props carry continuity (the clock at 8:56, the book, the card drawer).
- The world now has a second place: the street outside, walked at night in ep-007. A new
  environment is a `SETS` builder, not a constraint — but each one costs a full lighting and
  framing pass, so don't build one per reel.
- Genre rotation: after a quiet comedy, go colder — work, money, or a failure. After ep-007's
  failure, go to self-respect or plain reality; do not stack a third library-night in a row.
- Cast rotation: 1–2 characters is normal; 3 only when the third one has one line that lands. A solo reel
  works when the absence is the point (ep-010) — don't use it to skip writing the second person.
- Every episode has exactly one prop that matters (bus timetable, phone, untouched coffee, a
  hoodie, an open book).
- Title is a time or a small instruction, lower case: "eleven minutes", "leave it on",
  "four minutes", "dry it", "still open", "before ten".
- Time of day is now a rotation axis, not a constant: night (001–009) and dawn (010) both work. Vary it.

1. **The second chance at the vending machine** — a man keeps getting his snack back with a note.
2. **Two strangers share an umbrella for six blocks** — neither says a word until the corner.
3. **The kid who waves at every car** — one car stops.
4. **Grandmother's voicemail, still active** — he calls it to hear her say "leave a message".
