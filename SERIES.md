# Conversations Nobody Forgets

A recurring series. Two blocky people end up in the same place at the same time, and one
sentence stops the scroll. The default home is a small branch library that closes at nine.

## Format

- 22–30 s, 9:16, one location per episode, one conversation, 12–18 spoken lines.
- Cold open on the situation already in progress. No setup, no establishing wide, first line
  by 0.3 s.
- Every line also reads on screen in a comment card that points at whoever is talking
  (Instagram). YouTube gets the clean plate plus the `.srt`.
- Ends on a line that reframes everything before it, spoken over the frame the reel opened
  on, so the last frame meets the first. The title is the last line.
- One prop per episode does the work (a book, a card drawer, a clock).

They never discuss what happened last time. Continuity lives in objects — the book, the card
drawer, the clock at 8:56 — not in dialogue. Either one can carry a reel alone.

## Theme rotation (never two in a row)

loneliness · relationships · childhood · failure · discipline · self-respect · reality

## Episode shape

| beat | time | job |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–2.5 | hook: a specific, odd detail and the first contradiction |
| 2 | 2.5–9.0 | escalation, the other person reveals a stake |
| 3 | 9.0–15.0 | the turn — the room goes quiet for one beat |
| 4 | 15.0–24.0 | she answers it with an action, not a speech |
| 5 | 21.0–end | final line, then silence, then the mirrored first frame |

## Recurring sets

`library` · `bus_stop` · `bedroom` · `street_night` · `laundromat` · `diner`

## Rules

- Characters never explain their feelings. The camera and the pause do it.
- No jokes that need a caption to land.
- Every episode must be publishable on its own; the series is a frame, not a requirement.
- The final line must still work if the viewer has seen zero previous episodes.
