## Script

1. She unlocks a door nobody is waiting behind.
2. Every light in the room. Even the one over her desk.
3. The wheel has squeaked since 2019. She's never fixed it.
4. _(silence — the door swings out, the sign turns to OPEN)_
5. Nobody comes before ten.
6. She opens at seven.

One person, one room, one hour before it matters. She unlocks at 6:52, turns on every light,
pushes a trolley with a wheel that has squeaked for seven years — for a morning where the
first visitor is three hours away. The last card answers the first: nobody is waiting, and she
opens at seven anyway. Standards are what you keep when nobody is watching. Title spoken in
the final line; the reel cuts back to the shot it opened on, so it loops as the next morning.
