## What bit us (for the next upload)

- **Instagram:** after the caption, click the **video preview** to dismiss the hashtag
  suggestions — clicking the page backdrop instead silently drops the post with no error.
  Then click Share and wait for "Your reel has been shared."; the upload takes 1–3 min for
  a 30–40 MB file and the button does nothing visible until it lands.
- **YouTube Studio:** hashtags in the description open a suggestions dropdown that
  intercepts clicks on the Visibility tab. Press Escape first, then click the tab.
- The web upload flow has **no cover picker** for reels; Instagram picks its own frame.
