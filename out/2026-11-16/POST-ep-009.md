## Script

1. dev — You followed me.
2. meera — You took the long way.
3. dev — That's not an answer.
4. dev — Nobody comes out here.
5. meera — You did.
6. dev — Your spot.
7. meera — Twelve years.
8. meera — Of sitting here after the late shift.
9. dev — Alone.
10. meera — With the fire.
11. dev — So it's not my clearing.
12. meera — It's nobody's.
13. meera — This is my clearing. You can share it.

A clearing in the jungle, a bonfire at their feet, the moon and the stars over a gap in the
canopy. Dev thinks she followed him out here. She has been sitting on this log after the late
shift for twelve years — the bark is worn pale where she sits. The last card answers the
first: it is not his clearing and not hers, and she offers it anyway. Title spoken in the
final line; the reel cuts back to the shot it opened on, so it loops as the next night.
