## Script

1. dev — Your light was on.
2. meera — I leave it on.
3. dev — Why.
4. meera — So you'd know which one's mine.
5. dev — I know which one's yours.
6. meera — Which.
7. dev — Forty.
8. meera — You count them.
9. dev — Every night.
10. meera — How many are lit.
11. dev — Sixty-two.
12. meera — And yours.
13. dev — Mine's not on the list.
14. meera — Ninety-one flats. Yours is the dark one.

He counts the lit windows of the society from her balcony, every night, out of a notebook.
She leaves her light on so he knows which flat is hers — flat forty. The last card turns it
around: he is the one flat that is dark, because he is standing on her balcony counting
everyone else's. Title spoken in the final line; the reel cuts back to the shot it opened on.
